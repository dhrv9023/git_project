Engine Architecture

The Three Quantitative Pillars

Instead of naive point-in-time price guesses, StockBuddy operates on statistically defensible quantitative pillars.

K-Means Feature Clustering (6 Regimes)

Financial markets continuously shift between volatility regimes and structural trends. We map each trading day into a 5-dimensional feature space (RSI, EMA slope, MACD hist, Volatility, 10d Return) and apply K-Means clustering.

  • ✓ No Hard-Coded Rules: Regimes emerge naturally from mathematical cluster distance.
  • ✓ 6 Semantic States: Ranging from Trending Bull (0) to High Volatility Stress (5).
  • ✓ Noise-Resistant: Captures multi-indicator momentum & volatility alignment.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Why regimes instead of price predictions?+
Stock prices on efficient markets are near-random walk processes. However, technical indicator states (momentum, volatility, trend) do exhibit autocorrelation over short windows. Classifying the current state and showing historical odds for that state is statistically honest — unlike a single price forecast.
How are the 6 regimes defined?+
K-Means clusters on 5 features: RSI deviation from 50, normalized MACD histogram, EMA slope, 20-day volatility (std of log-returns), and 10-day price return. Clusters are then semantically ordered by a score combining momentum and volatility, mapping them to: Trending Bull, Overbought/Exhaustion, Sideways/Choppy, Recovery/Bounce, Downtrend/Bear, and High Volatility/Stress.
What does the Risk Condition Score mean?+
It's a 0–10 composite of: volatility stress (current vol vs. 90-day average), RSI extremity (distance from 50), trend misalignment (Close below EMA20), and MACD divergence (histogram negative). Score 0–3 = calm/bullish conditions; 7–10 = elevated risk. It's smoothed over 5 days to reduce daily noise.
Is this financial advice?+
Absolutely not. StockBuddy is an educational and research tool. Historical regime odds inform probability — they are not guarantees. Always consult a registered investment advisor before making any investment decisions.
Can I use my own data?+
Yes — the backend pipeline accepts any OHLCV dataframe. The regime engine can be enhanced with India VIX, FII/DII flow data, sector rotation data, and earnings calendars to produce richer regime classification.
